# Style

Follow the canonical Nyx style in the pinned `nyx-design` dependency (Design 2.0).
Reuse `nyx-theme`, semantic colors and GTK4 widgets. The popup is a solid
`@theme_popover` card (radius 15, a `@theme_border` hairline, no frosted glass), 560 px
wide, as tall as its rows up to 520 px, then scrolling. No toolbar, sidebar or status
bar: a search field, the Recent / Pinned / Snippets segmented control, the list, and
one line of key hints.
